Tarot Journaling FAQ

Can I edit these templates in Canva?

Yes! Many of the journal and planner suites are provided as Canva template links or high-res PNG files with transparent backgrounds. You can easily insert text and graphics using a free Canva account.

What paper weight is best for printing custom decks at home?

For printing DIY cards or planner pages, we recommend heavy cardstock with a density of 300 gsm (110 lb) or higher. A subtle linen or matte finish gives the graphics a luxurious, tactile feel.

How can I use SVG files for my journal?

SVG files are perfect for creating custom stickers, vinyl decals for your journal cover, or creating stencils using cutting machines like a Cricut or Silhouette.
