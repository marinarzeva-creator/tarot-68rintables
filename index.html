Bridal Stationery FAQ

Can I edit these tarot journal templates in Canva?

Yes! Many of the tarot journal and planner suites are provided as Canva template links or high-res PDF/PNG files. You can effortlessly add your names, wedding date, or change text using a free Canva account.

What paper weight is best for printing tarot cards at home?

For printing DIY tarot cards or tarot guest book pages, we recommend heavy cardstock with a density of 300 gsm (110 lb) or higher. A subtle linen or matte finish gives the graphics a luxurious, tactile feel.

Should I print tarot welcome boards at home or commercially?

For smaller items like table numbers, place cards, and journal prompts, printing at home on a quality inkjet printer works wonderfully. For large seating charts or welcome signs (18x24 inches or larger), upload your exported SVG/PDF file to a local print shop or online printer.
